# Privacy Policy

## 3D Chart Viz

Last updated: September 2026

3D Chart Viz is a Tableau visualization extension designed to display data provided through Tableau as interactive 3D visualizations.

## Data Collection

3D Chart Viz does not intentionally collect, store, sell, or share personal information.

The extension processes data made available by Tableau in order to render the visualization.

## Data Storage

3D Chart Viz does not intentionally store Tableau data on an external server.

Visualization data is used only for the purpose of rendering the chart during the current Tableau session.

## External Services

The extension may rely on services required to host or deliver the extension itself.

The developer does not intentionally transmit Tableau worksheet data to third-party services for advertising, analytics, profiling, or resale.

## Personal Information

The extension does not intentionally request personal information such as:

- Name
- Email address
- Postal address
- Phone number
- Account credentials
- Payment information

## Tableau Data

Users are responsible for ensuring that the data used with this extension complies with their organization's security, privacy, and data governance policies.

## Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as the extension evolves.

Any significant changes will be reflected in this document.
